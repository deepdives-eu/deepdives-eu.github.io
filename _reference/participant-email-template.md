# Participant thank-you email template

Maintainers' reference, not published on the site. Send after a course to thank participants, point them to the course page and remind them about the AWS feedback forms. Based on the email sent after Advanced Generative AI Development on AWS in October 2026, including the instructor's edits.

How to use: fill in the placeholders in curly braces, put participants in Bcc and yourself in To, keep the tone short and light. AWS expects feedback from at least 80 percent of participants, which is why the reminder stays in.

Placeholders: {COURSE NAME}, {NUMBER OF DAYS}, {COURSE PAGE URL}.

---

Subject: Thanks for joining {COURSE NAME}

Hi everyone,

Thank you for {NUMBER OF DAYS} good days of {COURSE NAME}. I enjoyed the questions and discussions, and I hope you left with a few ideas to try on your own projects.

As promised, here is the page with additional material for the course, organised module by module with the links from the slides plus extra documentation, blog posts, videos and the matching AWS Skill Builder courses and labs:

{COURSE PAGE URL}

One small favour: if you have not yet filled in the course feedback at https://myclass.skillbuilder.aws/, please take a few minutes to do so. There is a short form that asks for feedback one on the course content and one on me as the instructor. AWS counts on hearing from at least 80 percent of each class, and you would be saving your instructor from a stern email.

Good luck, and hope to see you in another course!

Best regards,
Johannes

---

Course page URL pattern: https://deepdives-eu.github.io/AWS/{PageFileNameWithoutExtension}
